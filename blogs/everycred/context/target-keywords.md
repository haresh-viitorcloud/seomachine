## LSI Keywords (Latent Semantic Indexing)

LSI keywords are semantically related terms that search engines use to confirm topical authority. Including them naturally in content — without forcing them — signals to Google and AI engines that an article covers a topic comprehensively. These are not keywords to target directly; they are terms to weave into the article body where they arise naturally.

### How to Use This Section

When writing an article targeting a specific cluster, pull LSI terms from:
1. The **Universal LSI** list (applies to every article)
2. The **Cluster-Specific LSI** section matching the primary keyword cluster
3. One or two **Industry-Specific LSI** groups matching the article's audience
4. Any relevant **Regulatory and Standards LSI** terms

Do not force all of them into every article. Use what arises naturally from the topic.

## Writing Rules for Target Keywords

### Keyword Frequency Requirement
Every keyword provided for a specific article must appear **at least 4 times** in the article. Distribute naturally — never repeat the same phrase in consecutive sentences or in the same paragraph twice.

### Natural Variation
When the same keyword would repeat awkwardly, use these approved variations:

| Target Keyword | Acceptable Variations |
|---------------|----------------------|
| verifiable credentials | digital credentials, W3C credentials, VCs, credential verification |
| digital identity | identity verification, digital ID, identity management |
| blockchain credentials | blockchain-anchored credentials, cryptographically signed credentials |
| digital id cards | digital officer IDs, mobile credentials, digital identification |
| digital trust platform | trust infrastructure, digital trust services |
| compliance automation software | automated compliance tools, compliance verification system |
| credentials verification | credential verification, instant verification |
| decentralized identity | self-sovereign identity, SSI, DID-based identity |

### One Primary Keyword Per Article
Each article should target one primary keyword and 2–3 supporting keywords from the same cluster. Avoid targeting keywords from multiple clusters in a single article — it dilutes focus.

### Keyword in Required Locations
Primary keyword must appear in:
- H1 title
- First 100 words
- At least 2 H2 subheadings
- Meta title
- Meta description
- URL slug

## Anti-Cannibalization Notes

These keyword pairs are similar enough that two articles targeting both could compete against each other. Write one article per pair, not two:

- "verifiable credentials" and "verified credentials" → target together in one article; do not split
- "digital credential platform" and "credential platform" → target together; one article covers both
- "blockchain identity solutions" and "blockchain identity verification" → one article per topic maximum
- "digital trust platform" and "digital trust services" → one article; cover both as related concepts
- "digital government transformation" and "digital government solutions" → pair these in the same article; they attract the same audience
