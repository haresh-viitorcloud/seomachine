## Content Formats That Build AI Citation Authority

These content formats earn the most citations from AI tools when users ask about museum ticketing:

| Format | Why AI Cites It | EveryTicket Action |
|---|---|---|
| Original data and statistics | AI cites sources of primary data | Publish annual/quarterly data reports: tickets processed, online booking growth, wait time reduction at Indian museums |
| Step-by-step implementation guides | AI pulls structured how-to content | Keep publishing and updating the 30-day deployment guide, migration guide, and timed entry setup guide |
| Comparison and "vs" content | AI cites balanced comparisons | Publish "online ticketing vs manual counters" and "flat fee vs per-ticket fee" comparison articles (no competitor names) |
| FAQ pages and schema | AI pulls FAQ schema directly | Add FAQ schema markup to all blog articles — these appear in Google PAA and are scraped by AI engines |
| Case studies with specific numbers | AI cites named, quantified outcomes | Publish the MAP Bangalore case study as a standalone page with specific numbers (tickets processed, wait time before/after, setup timeline) |
