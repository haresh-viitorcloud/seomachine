# EveryTicket Features & Benefits

This document outlines EveryTicket's key features, benefits, and differentiators to inform content creation that drives demo bookings and customer acquisition.

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. **All-in-One Ticketing Platform**
- **Feature**: Combines online booking, counter POS, self-service kiosks, CRM, and analytics in a single unified dashboard — no separate tools needed.
- **Benefit**: Museums manage every sales channel and every visitor touchpoint from one place, eliminating data silos and manual reconciliation.
- **Conversion Angle**: "One platform for every ticket — online, counter, and kiosk — with real-time sync across all channels."

### 2. **Online Ticketing & Booking Portal**
- **Feature**: Branded, mobile-friendly online booking portal where visitors purchase tickets in advance, select time slots, and receive digital tickets with barcodes — all without staff involvement.
- **Benefit**: Museums sell tickets 24/7, reduce counter queues, and capture visitor data automatically.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Start selling online in 60 minutes — your visitors book, pay, and receive tickets without ever joining a queue."

### 3. **POS (Point of Sale) for Walk-In Visitors**
- **Feature**: Counter-based POS software supporting UPI, card, and cash payments, with GST-compliant billing, automated receipts, and offline mode (auto-syncs when connection restores).
- **Benefit**: Counter staff process walk-in tickets faster; finance teams get accurate daily settlement reports without manual tally.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Your counters process more visitors with less effort — and your accounts team gets auto-reconciled daily reports."

### 4. **Timed Entry & Capacity Management**
- **Feature**: Visitors select specific arrival time slots during booking; the system automatically stops sales when a slot reaches maximum capacity, preventing overbooking.
- **Benefit**: Eliminates morning rushes, distributes footfall evenly across operating hours, and enables security teams to plan staffing in advance.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Reduce visitor wait times by up to 40% — EveryTicket distributes your crowd before they even arrive."

### 5. **QR Code Scanning & Entry Management**
- **Feature**: Digital tickets carry unique QR/barcodes scanned at entry gates in under one second, with real-time validation and occupancy tracking visible to staff.
- **Benefit**: Eliminates fake or duplicate tickets, speeds up entry, and gives managers a live count of visitors on-premises at any moment.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Scan, verify, and admit visitors in under a second — no paper, no fraud, no bottlenecks."

### 6. **Built-In CRM & Visitor Profiles**
- **Feature**: Captures visitor identity, booking history, preferences, and communication records in an integrated CRM; supports membership tracking and segmented communication.
- **Benefit**: Museums build lasting visitor relationships, drive repeat visits, and run targeted campaigns from the same platform used to sell tickets.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Turn every ticket purchase into a visitor profile — and every profile into a repeat visit."

### 7. **Self-Service Kiosks**
- **Feature**: Touchscreen kiosk hardware running EveryTicket software — visitors purchase tickets on-site without joining a manned counter queue; prints tickets instantly.
- **Benefit**: Reduces counter staffing pressure during peak hours while giving visitors a fast, modern entry experience.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Your busiest days get easier — kiosks handle walk-in sales while your staff focuses on the visitor experience."

### 8. **GST-Ready Billing & Indian Tax Compliance**
- **Feature**: All tickets generate GST-compliant invoices automatically; the platform is fully aligned with Indian tax requirements including UPI and Indian payment gateway standards.
- **Benefit**: Finance teams avoid manual GST calculation and audit risk; procurement officers can confidently deploy the system without custom compliance work.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Built for India — every transaction is GST-compliant out of the box, with UPI support from day one."

## Common Questions & Objections

### "How long does implementation take?"
**Answer**: Your online booking portal goes live in 60 minutes. Full deployment including POS, staff training, payment gateway setup, and integration testing follows a 30-day framework — with live operations typically starting in week four.

### "What happens if the internet goes down at our counters?"
**Answer**: EveryTicket's POS operates in offline mode during connectivity loss. All transactions are logged locally and automatically synced to the central dashboard when the connection is restored — no data is lost.

### "Is EveryTicket compliant with Indian tax requirements?"
**Answer**: Yes. All transactions generate GST-compliant invoices automatically. The platform supports UPI payments natively and is built to Indian regulatory standards — no custom compliance configuration is required.

### "Do you charge per ticket sold?"
**Answer**: No. EveryTicket charges a flat monthly subscription. There are no per-ticket commissions, which means your revenue scales without your software costs scaling with it.

### "Can we use our own branding on the booking portal?"
**Answer**: Yes. Essential, Institutional, and Custom plans include a fully custom-branded booking portal with your museum's logo, colors, and domain.

### "Does it work for events and not just regular admissions?"
**Answer**: Yes. EveryTicket supports event-specific ticketing alongside regular admissions — you can create timed or open-entry events, festivals, workshops, and special exhibitions all within the same platform.
