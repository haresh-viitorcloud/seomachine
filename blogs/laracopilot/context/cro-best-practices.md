# CRO Best Practices for LaraCopilot Content

Conversion guidance for LaraCopilot blog posts and landing content. The goal is trial sign-ups from a skeptical developer audience that distrusts marketing.

## Primary Conversion Goals
1. **Start free** (sign up): https://laracopilot.com/sign-page/ — the main CTA.
2. **Start a 14-day free trial** of a paid plan (no credit card required).
3. **Talk to Sales** for Enterprise: https://laracopilot.com/enterprise/.

## CTA Rules
- Lead value first, ask second. Earn the click with a useful article, then invite the next step.
- Place the first CTA within the first ~500 words, one mid-article, one strong CTA at the end.
- Use specific, low-friction copy: "Get started free", "Build your first feature in minutes", "Try it on your own codebase", "Start your 14-day free trial — no credit card".
- Match the CTA to the content: comparison post → "See how LaraCopilot handles the backend"; how-to → "Generate this with LaraCopilot"; founder post → "Validate your idea fast".
- Never use "Click here". Use descriptive anchors.

## What Converts a Developer Audience
- **Proof through real code** — show actual Laravel output, not claims.
- **Honesty** — admit limits and when a competitor fits; credibility drives conversion here.
- **Free tier + no-card trial** — emphasize the zero-risk entry (Free plan: 10 credits, 2 projects).
- **Ownable code, no lock-in** — a key objection-killer; repeat it.
- **Works with existing repos** — connect GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket lowers switching cost.
- **One-click deploy** — the "from chat to production" payoff.

## Objection Handling (weave into content)
- "AI doesn't understand Laravel" → Laravel-native intelligence, real Eloquent/Artisan/Filament output.
- "I'll have to rewrite it" → standard, tested, ownable Laravel code.
- "Is my code safe?" → sent to OpenAI/Anthropic for processing only, never stored or trained on, encrypted in transit and at rest.
- "Too expensive" → Free plan + 14-day trial; transparent credit-based pricing.
- "Frontend builders can't do my backend" → relationships, authorization, queues, APIs, multi-tenancy.

## Trust Elements to Include
- On-site testimonials (named): James Rodriguez, Sarah Park, Marco Kühn.
- Concrete capabilities and integrations (Laravel Cloud, Forge, Ploi, GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket).
- Accurate pricing and trial terms.
- Do NOT invent stats, user counts, or "X% faster" figures — unverifiable claims erode developer trust.

## Page/Section Hygiene
- Clear single primary CTA per section; avoid competing asks.
- Comparison tables and code blocks reduce friction by answering questions in place.
- Keep forms/sign-up framing simple: free, no credit card, minutes to first result.
