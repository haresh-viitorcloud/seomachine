## How to Read This Document

- **Volume** = average monthly searches, last 12 months. `IN` = India, `Global` = all countries.
- **KD** (Ahrefs Keyword Difficulty, 0–100): **≤10 quick win** · **11–25 moderate** · **>25 hard** · **blank = Ahrefs has not computed it**, which usually signals very low competition rather than missing opportunity.
- **CPC** = average advertiser cost-per-click, USD. Higher CPC = stronger commercial/buyer intent. In this niche CPC is a better priority signal than volume.
- **Priority**: P1 = work now · P2 = next · P3 = medium-term authority play.

> **Volume caveat from the research (important).** Search volumes in this niche are modest *by design*. This is high-ticket B2B and government work where a single ranking can be worth a tender invitation. **Judge keywords by intent and deal value, not raw traffic.** A 100/mo keyword at $0.50 CPC can outrank a 5,000/mo informational term in business value.

## Cluster 9: The Destination Play (Case-Study Keywords)

A distinct, high-yield strategy copied from Tagbin: their case-study pages rank for the **names of the attractions they built** — "prime minister museum" (1,400/mo), "war memorial dharamshala" (1,000/mo) — all at **KD 0–4**.

ViitorX has the same raw material and should ship every project with a search-optimised case-study page:
- Varanasi Ropeway · NIA holographic digital twin · Statue of Unity · CSMVS exhibition · Adani Centre of Excellence · G20 Khajuraho · Kaziranga · Swachh Bharat

**Corporate equivalent**: "[client brand] experience centre" searches already have demand — e.g. **"duroflex experience centre" 1,600/mo**, **"kohler experience center" 400/mo**. Create one page per corporate build, **with client consent**.

This is the cheapest traffic in the entire strategy: the demand already exists, competition is near zero, and the searchers are exactly the institutional buyers who commission the next project.

## Usage Guidelines

### When writing new content
1. Identify the cluster, then target **one** pillar or cluster keyword — never two from the same cluster on one page.
2. Check the Cannibalisation Watch before creating anything in the experience-centre or exhibition clusters.
3. Anchor every piece to a real case study (there are 33 published) — this is what earns AI Overview citations.
4. Judge the keyword by **CPC and intent**, not volume. See the volume caveat above.
5. Cross-link to the cluster pillar and to `/case-studies/`.

### When optimising existing content
1. The 29 existing blog posts already cover much of the P1/P2 informational set — **audit before creating**. Optimising an existing post usually beats writing a new one.
2. Positioning-cluster terms belong on the homepage and offering pages, not blog posts.

### Structured for AI Overviews
Competitors (Simulanis, Fusion VR, DevDen, CHRP) currently earn free AI Overview visibility on VR-training queries. Every guide should carry structured, citable case data — specific projects, specific numbers, specific outcomes — to compete for those citations.
