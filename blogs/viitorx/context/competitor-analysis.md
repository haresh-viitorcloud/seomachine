## The Three Competitor Playbooks

The research identified three distinct, repeatable strategies working in this market. All three are available to ViitorX.

### 1. Category-cluster play — *Fusion VR, Simulanis*
Their homepages rank #1–5 for the entire "VR / AR-VR company in India" cluster (~30 variants, mostly KD under 30) **with very little supporting content**. The homepage does the work.

→ **ViitorX's homepage and four offering pages can compete directly.** Cheapest, fastest win available.

### 2. Destination play — *Tagbin*
Case-study pages rank for the **names of the attractions built** — all KD 0–4, with demand that already exists.

→ **ViitorX has identical raw material**: Varanasi Ropeway, NIA digital twin, Statue of Unity, CSMVS, Adani CoE, G20 Khajuraho, Kaziranga, Swachh Bharat. Corporate equivalent: "[brand] experience centre" searches ("duroflex experience centre" 1,600/mo; "kohler experience center" 400/mo).
→ **Every project should ship with a search-optimised case-study page.**

### 3. Thought-leadership gap — *nobody owns it*
Tagbin holds #1 for "experiential marketing" (1,200/mo) with **one blog post**. "virtual reality development" (2,300/mo, KD 4) has **no strong owner**.

→ Pillar guides win these outright, and they earn Google AI Overview citations — where Simulanis, Fusion VR, DevDen and CHRP currently get free visibility.

## ViitorX's Competitive Advantages

Grounded in verifiable facts — see `context/features.md`.

1. **Breadth no single competitor matches** — Tagbin does government/museums, Fusion VR and Simulanis do industrial VR, Noomo does 3D web. ViitorX credibly does all four.
2. **Measurement built into every offering** — dwell time, attention analytics, competency scoring. Competitors sell the experience; ViitorX sells the evidence.
3. **Institutional proof at the top tier** — G20, Noida International Airport, MAP, CSMVS, Swachh Bharat Mission.
4. **33 published case studies** — more citable, structured proof than most of this set combined.
5. **Hardware partnership rather than hardware dependency** — the Opezee split lets ViitorX stay hardware-neutral.
6. **134 referring domains at launch** — authority already banked, not yet converted.
7. **ISO 27001 certification** — matters for public procurement, where Tagbin competes.
8. **ViitorCloud group backing** — 10+ years of delivery infrastructure behind a new brand.

### Their advantages over ViitorX
1. **Time in the index** — every competitor has years of crawl history; ViitorX has none.
2. **Dart Design's 814 referring domains** and Noomo's DR 51.
3. **Incumbent AI Overview citations** — Simulanis, Fusion VR, DevDen, CHRP are already the default answer.
4. **Tagbin's government track record** is longer and more visible in search.
