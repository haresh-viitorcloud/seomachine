# Change.org — Publishing Guide (for Repurposed Content)

**URL:** https://www.change.org
**Type:** **Petition platform — not a blogging site.** You don't publish articles here.
**Repurposing verdict:** ❗ **Only relevant for advocacy/cause content.** You cannot paste a normal blog article and expect it to fit. If a piece of yours argues for a change, you can adapt it into a **petition** (and post **petition updates**). For everything else, Change.org isn't the right channel — and it's *not* a backlink/SEO tool.

Change.org's own organizing toolkit actually recommends publishing a blog post or op-ed **elsewhere** and linking to the petition — so treat Change.org as something your articles *point to*, not a place to republish them.

---

## When it makes sense

Use Change.org only when the source article is about a problem with a clear ask — something a named decision maker could act on. Distill the article into a short, persuasive, solution-focused petition.

---

## How to start a petition

1. Go to Change.org → **Start a petition** (free, a few minutes).
2. **Describe your issue.**
3. **Choose an approach:** AI-assisted drafting (default) or **"Write your own"** for a blank form you fill in yourself.
4. **Title** — name the *solution*, not just the problem. Use action verbs (**stop, save, ban, change, oppose, add, start**), include key names/places, keep it concise, and avoid ALL CAPS or `!!!`.
5. **Story** — explain the problem, why it matters, and the impact of the solution. **Keep it ~150–400 words**; shorter, focused stories perform better. Short paragraphs, optional headings (Who/What/Where/When/Why/How), and a clear call to action.
6. **Image or video** — this is the first thing people see and the social-share preview. Emotive close-ups of people/animals work well.
7. **Scope** — local, national, or global; pick a **topic**.
8. **Decision makers** — add the people/orgs who can actually make the change. **Keep it under 10.**
9. **Review → Publish.**

> A petition needs **at least 5 signatures** before it appears in Change.org search; until then only people with the direct link can find it.

---

## Posting updates (the closest thing to "posts")

Once live, use updates to keep supporters engaged:

- Logged in, choose **"Update supporters"** (top of the petition) or scroll to the bottom and click **"Post an Update."**
- Write the update and publish. It becomes **public on the petition page** and **emails all current signers**. Each update can include **one image or link preview**.
- Post updates for milestones, media coverage, meetings with decision makers, setbacks, and calls to action.

This is where you might lightly repurpose a paragraph or two from an article — as an update that adds news or a call to action, not as a wholesale re-post.

## Writing instructions for Change.org (petition, not a blog post)

- **Title:** name the **solution**, lead with an action verb (stop / save / ban / change / add), and include the decision maker and who's affected. Keep it concise; skip ALL CAPS and "!!!".
- **Story (~150–400 words, shorter performs better):** problem → why it matters → the **specific ask** → the impact if it's granted. Short paragraphs; optional Who/What/Where/When/Why/How headings; end with one clear call to action.
- **Tone:** personal and credible — first-person, backed by facts and links to trustworthy sources.
- **Media:** one emotive image or video (it's also the social-share preview).
- **Updates:** each update = a piece of news + a call to action, with a single image or link.
- **Reminder:** repurpose only genuinely advocacy-oriented material here — distill it, don't paste an article.

---

## Metadata & SEO tags

Change.org is a petition platform — you don't get blog-style meta fields. The only meta-like levers:

- **Petition title** → used as the page / OG title in search and social shares.
- **Petition image or video** → the social-share (OG) image.
- **Story opening** → may be used as the share snippet.

There's **no meta-description, custom slug, or canonical control** to speak of. This isn't an SEO channel — see the note above about linking *to* the petition from your own blog/op-eds rather than the other way around.

---

## Repurposing checklist for Change.org

- [ ] The source content is genuinely advocacy/cause-based (otherwise skip Change.org)
- [ ] Adapted into a **solution-focused title** + **150–400 word story** — not a pasted article
- [ ] Relevant image/video added
- [ ] Scope + topic set; **under 10** decision makers
- [ ] Published and pushing toward the first **5 signatures**
- [ ] Plan to link **to** this petition from your blog/op-eds elsewhere (not the other way around)
