## How publishing generally works

1. **Sign up** for an account (`theomnibuzz.com/signup`).
2. **Create / submit an article**, choosing the **relevant category/niche**.
3. **Include an image** (expected for publishing).
4. Submission goes through **review**, then publishes in the niche-relevant section (turnaround is usually fast).

## Content guidelines (as stated for this platform)

- **Unique, well-written, plagiarism-free**, with **no grammatical errors**.
- **Max ~2 backlinks** per post.
- **Image required/recommended.**
- Deeply-researched, original-feeling articles fit the platform's "quality-gated" positioning best.

---

## Handling the re-post (rework, don't paste)

No canonical back to your site, and the platform asks for unique/plagiarism-free content:

- **Rewrite into a distinct version** — fresh intro, reworked structure, different examples. Don't paste the exact article that's already live on your site.
- Include up to **two contextual links** back to your original/site, placed naturally.
- Give this site its **own** version — don't reuse the identical reworded copy on other guest platforms.

## Blog-writing instructions for TheOmniBuzz

- **Positioning:** "quality-gated," expert content — write deeply-researched, genuinely useful articles in the right niche category (Tech, AI, Business, Finance, Digital Marketing, Health, etc.).
- **Originality:** unique, plagiarism-free, and error-free (rewrite repurposed pieces).
- **Structure:** a specific headline, a strong intro, scannable subheads, short paragraphs, and at least one image.
- **Length:** substantial — aim ~800–1,500+ words.
- **Links:** **≤ 2** contextual backlinks to your site; value-first, not promotional.

## Metadata & SEO tags

- Supply a **meta title** (~60 chars) and **meta description** (~155 chars) with your keyword; the platform/editor may set the final values.
- Include an **image** (used as the OG/social image).
- Suggest a keyword-rich **slug** if the submission allows.
- **Canonical** points to OmniBuzz, not you — so use a reworded version plus a contextual backlink.
- ⚠️ Confirm which meta fields the current submission flow actually exposes.
