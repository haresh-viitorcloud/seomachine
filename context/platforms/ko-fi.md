# Ko-fi — Publishing Guide (for Repurposed Content)

**URL:** https://ko-fi.com
**Type:** Creator support platform (tips, memberships, shop) that also works as a content publishing space.
**Repurposing verdict:** ⚠️ **Fine for your audience, not for SEO.** No canonical option, but Ko-fi isn't a serious search competitor for your original. Post your work freely; lead readers back to your site and to supporting you.

---

## Account

Create a free Ko-fi page (takes ~60 seconds to personalize). Optional **Ko-fi Gold** upgrade adds features.

## Post types

| Type | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Blog Post** | Longer articles (your main option) | Has its own URL, **no character limit**, can be scheduled, edited after publishing, and restricted to supporters |
| Quick Update | Short notes | Shows in full on your page but **no dedicated URL**, and **can't be edited** after posting (only deleted) |
| Image / Video / Audio | Media-led posts | Spotlights the uploaded media |

For repurposed articles, use **Blog Post**.

---

## How to publish a blog post

1. Go to your **Dashboard** and create a **Post** → choose **Blog Post**.
2. **Title** — also becomes the post's link and the social-share headline.
3. **Body content** — paste your article; use the toolbar for styling, links, and embeds.
4. **Featured image** — via the browse button. Best at **2:1 ratio (~600 × 300px)**; shows at the top, in previews, and on social shares. (If you skip it, Ko-fi supplies a default preview.)
5. **Embedded content URL** — optional (YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, TikTok, Twitch, etc.).
6. **Tags** — optional.
7. Set **visibility**: public, or **supporters-only** (one-off and/or monthly supporters).
8. **Publish** now or **schedule** for later.

### Working details

- **Links are not auto-clickable** — select the text, click the **Link** icon, paste the URL (tick "Open in new tab" if you like).
- **Images:** JPG/JPEG/PNG/GIF, **under 10MB**; you can resize, caption, add alt-text, and link them. Uploaded images also land in your gallery.
- **Files:** share via a Dropbox/Google Drive link using the link feature.

## Handling the re-post (no canonical here)

Ko-fi doesn't expose a canonical field, so pick one:

- **Full re-post (simplest):** paste the whole article. Low SEO risk because Ko-fi pages aren't competing hard for your keywords. **Add a line linking to the original** on your site.
- **Teaser + link:** post the intro and a "read the full article on [your site]" link — keeps your site as the primary home.
- Either way, **end with a support call-to-action**, e.g. "Enjoyed this? You can support my work on Ko-fi." You can also give **supporters early access** to a post before it goes public.

## Sharing

Scroll to the bottom of a published post → click the link button to copy the URL, or publish straight to X/Facebook. Your featured image becomes the share preview.

## Blog-writing instructions for Ko-fi

- **Audience:** your fans and supporters — write warm, personal, and direct.
- **Front-load value:** only the start of a blog post shows on your page before a "read more," so make the opening lines pull people in.
- **Title:** clear and inviting; it also becomes the URL and the social headline.
- **Structure:** short paragraphs, images to break things up (add alt-text), and links made clickable via the link icon.
- **Length:** no limit — but keep it focused; supporters skim.
- **Ending:** a friendly support CTA ("If you enjoyed this, you can buy me a coffee / join my membership").
- **Extra:** consider giving supporters **early access** to a post before it goes public.

## Metadata & SEO tags

Ko-fi gives limited SEO-meta control — the practical levers are:

- **Title** → becomes the page title, the URL slug, and the social (OG) title. Make it clear and keyword-aware.
- **Featured image** → the OG/social-share image (2:1, ~600 × 300). Always set one.
- **Opening text** → often used as the social description, so front-load a clear summary.
- There's **no separate meta-description or canonical field.** If SEO matters for this piece, keep the original on your own site (canonical lives there) and treat the Ko-fi post as reach plus a link back.

## Repurposing checklist for Ko-fi

- [ ] Used **Blog Post** (not Quick Update — those can't be edited or linked to)
- [ ] Featured image at ~2:1, under 10MB
- [ ] Links made clickable via the link icon
- [ ] Link back to the original on your site
- [ ] Support CTA at the end
- [ ] Set visibility (public vs supporters-only)
