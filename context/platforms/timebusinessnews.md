# Time Business News — Publishing Guide (for Repurposed Content)

**URL:** https://timebusinessnews.com
**Type:** Business / tech / lifestyle news site that accepts guest contributions (categories include Business, Technology, Lifestyle, Economy, Health, Real Estate). Mid-range domain authority.
**Repurposing verdict:** ⚠️ **A backlink channel, not a syndication mirror.** No canonical-to-your-site. Guest/news sites like this generally expect content that reads as fresh, so **rework the article into a unique version** and include a **contextual link back** to your site.

> Heads-up: submission flow and pricing on sites like this change often, and some placements are paid. **Confirm the current "Write for Us" / contributor process on the site before building a routine around this.**

---

## How publishing generally works

Time Business News typically operates on a **contributor-account + editorial-review** model:

1. **Register / request a contributor (author) account**, or use the site's **"Write for Us" / submission** process.
2. **Submit your article** (through the dashboard or as directed).
3. **Editorial review** — the team checks it against their guidelines; you're notified when it's approved and goes live.
4. They **monitor published posts** and reserve the right to edit or remove content.

---

## Content guidelines (typical for this kind of site)

- **Quality & originality:** well-written, correct grammar, genuinely useful. Reworked/unique reads far better than a verbatim copy of something already indexed.
- **Niche relevance:** match one of their categories and their tone — read a few recent posts first.
- **Links:** you can usually include a **backlink to your site** (in the body and/or author bio). Keep links modest — **around two max** — and contextual, not spammy.
- **Add an image** to make the piece publish-ready.
- **Author bio:** short bio with your link and optionally social handles.

---

## Handling the re-post (rework, don't paste)

Because there's no canonical back to you and guest sites value freshness:

- **Rewrite into a distinct version** — new intro, reordered points, a different angle or example set. Aim for something Google reads as its own article.
- Keep **one contextual link** to the original/your site in a natural spot in the body.
- Don't submit the *same* reworded copy to multiple guest sites — give each its own version, or you risk duplicate issues across those sites too.

## Blog-writing instructions for Time Business News

- **Tone:** business / tech / lifestyle news style — informative and neutral, **not** an advertisement for your company.
- **Originality:** rewrite repurposed content into a genuinely unique version (guest/news sites reject verbatim copies).
- **Structure:** a clear, specific headline; a short intro that states the value; subheads; short paragraphs; and at least one relevant image.
- **Length:** aim ~700–1,500 words (confirm their current guideline).
- **Links:** keep to **~2** relevant, contextual links (often one in-body + one in the author bio); avoid promotional link-stuffing.
- **Credibility:** cite sources/data and keep any statistics recent.
- Include a short **author bio** with your link.

---

## Metadata & SEO tags

On most guest/news sites the **editor's CMS controls the final meta**, but supply your own so they have it:

- Provide a **suggested meta title** (~60 chars) and **meta description** (~155 chars), both with your target keyword.
- Suggest a short, keyword-rich **URL slug**.
- Supply a **featured image** (it becomes the OG/social image).
- **Canonical:** these sites point the canonical at their own URL — that's expected, since the value to you is the backlink, not canonical. So rework the piece to be unique and add a contextual link back.
- ⚠️ Whether the author dashboard exposes meta-title/description fields varies — confirm on the site.
