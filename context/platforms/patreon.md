# Patreon — Publishing Guide (for Repurposed Content)

**URL:** https://patreon.com
**Type:** Membership platform. Posts can be public or gated to paid/free members and specific tiers.
**Repurposing verdict:** ⚠️ **Great for gated/member content, weak for public SEO.** No canonical option. Best used to put a fuller/bonus version behind a paywall, with public teasers that link back to your site.

---

## Account / setup

Create a creator page and complete the launch checklist. **Patreon's Trust & Safety team reviews your page before it goes live.** You'll also categorize your page as **"Safe for All Audiences"** or **"Adult/18+"** (payment-processor requirement).

---

## How to publish a post

1. From the left navigation, click **Create → Post**.
2. Pick your post type using the options at the top of the editor, then start typing the body (description/update/article).
3. Add **inline media** as needed — images, video, audio, dividers, buttons, file attachments, or a **paywall** — via the **+** button in the text area.
4. In the **right-side panel**, set:
   - **Audience** — Public / Paid members (all or specific **tiers**) / Free members. This is the key control.
   - **Collection** and **tags**.
   - **Preview** settings (e.g. a preview clip for locked video/audio; unblur/crop a thumbnail shown to non-members).
   - **More options** — e.g. **early access** (set Audience to Paid first), comment settings, notifications.
5. **Publish now**, **schedule** (toggle *Set publish date* — you can also backdate), or leave as a **draft** (autosaved).

### How the paywall behaves

The paywall applies to **inline body content**. Header media visibility is controlled separately by Audience — so you can, for example, keep the intro and first images free while locking the rest of the article to paying members. You can insert a paywall into a public post to convert it into a paid post.

---

## Media specs

- Feed images display at **16:9** — size to **1920 × 1080px** for a clean look with no side bars.
- Thumbnails are **square (1:1)**.
- Add **alt-text and captions** to images for accessibility.

---

## Handling the re-post (no canonical here)

No canonical field, so play to Patreon's strength — gating:

- **Member-gated version:** put the full or an **expanded/bonus** version behind a paid tier. Gated content isn't a public duplicate, so duplicate-content worries mostly disappear.
- **Public teaser:** publish a short public version (great for discovery via Patreon's home feed / **Quips**) that **links back to the full article** on your site.
- Straight public full re-posts are allowed, but you get no SEO benefit and no gating value — usually the least useful option here.

## Blog-writing instructions for Patreon

- **Audience:** members and would-be members. Decide the post's job first — public discovery vs member reward — and write accordingly.
- **Public / teaser posts:** strong hook + real value, then "the full version is available to members," and link back to your site's original where relevant.
- **Member posts:** deliver depth, behind-the-scenes, or extras that justify the membership.
- **Title + thumbnail:** benefit-led title; square (1:1) thumbnail; for locked media set an enticing preview clip/image.
- **Paywall placement:** give a genuinely useful free intro, then insert the paywall so the preview earns the click.
- **Community:** invite comments to build engagement.

---

## Metadata & SEO tags

Patreon's SEO-meta control is limited, and **member-only posts aren't public**, so they carry no public meta at all. For **public** posts:

- **Title** → page / OG title.
- **Thumbnail (1:1)** and header media → the social-share image; set an enticing preview for any locked media.
- **Preview / first text** → the snippet used in shares.
- **No custom meta-description or canonical field** — for public teasers, keep your site's original as the canonical home and link to it.
- Patreon's own help has a page-level note, "How can I optimize my page for SEO?", worth a look.

## Repurposing checklist for Patreon

- [ ] Page already reviewed/live and correctly categorized (All Audiences vs 18+)
- [ ] Post created via **Create → Post**
- [ ] **Audience** set intentionally (public teaser vs paid/tier-gated)
- [ ] Paywall placed correctly if using a free intro + locked body
- [ ] Images at 16:9 (1920×1080), thumbnails square, alt-text added
- [ ] Public/teaser posts link back to the original on your site
