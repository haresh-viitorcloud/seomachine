# Buy Me a Coffee — Publishing Guide (for Repurposed Content)

**URL:** https://buymeacoffee.com
**Type:** Creator support platform (tips, memberships, shop) with a built-in Posts feature.
**Repurposing verdict:** ⚠️ **Fine for your audience, not for SEO.** No canonical option. Post freely for your followers, add a link back to your site, and use the built-in email blast to your followers.

---

## Account

Create a free creator account and **set up your payout info** (needed before some features work).

---

## How to publish a post

1. Go to your **Dashboard** → find **Post** in the left menu → click **Write a Post**.
2. Fill in the **title** and **description/body** in the customizable editor. Posts can be an article, image, or audio.
3. Choose **visibility**:
   - **Public** — visible to everyone.
   - **Supporters-only** — visible to supporters and members.
   - **Members-only** — visible only to members.
4. (Optional) **Add categories** to the post.
5. Decide when to publish: **publish now**, **schedule** for a later time, or **save as draft**.
6. (Optional) **Email the post to all your followers** — a built-in way to notify your audience.
7. Publish.

---

## Handling the re-post (no canonical here)

There's no canonical field, so:

- **Full re-post** is fine — the point of BMC is your audience, not search ranking. Include a **link back to the original** on your site.
- Or post a **teaser + link** if you'd rather keep all the SEO weight on your own site.
- Consider putting an **expanded or bonus version** behind supporters/members and keeping a **public teaser** that links out.

Follow the **Content Guidelines** (and, if you sell downloads, the **Product Guidelines**) on the site.

## Blog-writing instructions for Buy Me a Coffee

- **Audience:** your followers/supporters — personal and direct, like writing to a friend.
- Because you can **email the post to followers**, write an opening line that also works as an email hook.
- **Title + description:** benefit-led title; the description carries the article body.
- **Structure:** short paragraphs, at least one image, clear links; add a **category**.
- **CTA:** point readers to support you or join a membership tier.
- Consider a **members-only** expanded version plus a **public teaser** that links back to your original.

---

## Metadata & SEO tags

Similar to Ko-fi — limited dedicated meta fields:

- **Title** → page title, URL, and OG/social title.
- **Cover / first image** → OG/social image.
- **Description opening** → used for the social/preview snippet; front-load the summary (it also doubles as your follower-email hook).
- **No separate meta-description or canonical field** — keep the canonical version on your own site and link back.
