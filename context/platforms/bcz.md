# BCZ.com — Publishing Guide (for Repurposed Content)

**URL:** https://bcz.com
**Type:** Free website/blog creation platform ("Business Connection Zone"), WordPress-based, marketed for SEO / "Web 2.0" article submission. You **claim your own subdomain** — `[username].bcz.com` — and run it like a mini blog you control.
**Repurposing verdict:** ⚠️ **A Web 2.0 property you own — usually used for backlinks.** No native canonical unless an SEO plugin is available. **Rework or use a teaser**, and include a **contextual link back** to your main site.

> Heads-up: features and sign-up availability on BCZ can vary. **Confirm the current process on the site** before building it into a workflow.

---

## Set up your site (one-time)

1. Go to **bcz.com** and **claim a free site** — `[username].bcz.com`.
2. You get a **WordPress-style dashboard** to manage posts, images, and settings; BCZ handles hosting, backups, and updates.

---

## How to publish a post

1. Log in to your BCZ dashboard.
2. Create a **new post** (standard WordPress-style editor).
3. Add a **title** and **content**, insert **images**, and set **tags/categories**.
4. **Publish** (or save as a draft).

Because it's WordPress-based, the flow mirrors any WordPress blog: write, format, add media, categorize, publish.

---

## Handling the re-post (it's your property, used for links)

Since BCZ sites are typically used to build backlinks to a "money site," and there's no guaranteed canonical field:

- **Rework into a distinct version** — the safest option if you want the BCZ copy indexed without clashing with your original.
- **Or post a teaser + link** to the full article on your main site.
- Include a **contextual link back** to your site in the body (this backlink is usually the whole point).
- If an **SEO plugin / canonical option** is available in your dashboard, you can set a canonical to your original instead of rewriting.
- As with other guest/Web 2.0 sites, don't reuse the identical copy across several of them.

## Blog-writing instructions for BCZ.com

Treat it like a small WordPress blog you own — write for SEO **and** readers:

- Put your **keyword** in the title and early in the body; add descriptive image **alt-text**; include a natural, **contextual link back** to your main site.
- Use **categories/tags**.
- **Structure:** clear headline, a hook, subheads, short paragraphs, and at least one image.
- **Content mode:** rewrite into a unique version, or post a teaser that links to your full article.
- **Length:** ~500–1,000+ words works well for a Web 2.0 property.

---

## Metadata & SEO tags

BCZ is WordPress-based, so meta control depends on whether an SEO plugin (Yoast / Rank Math) is exposed in your dashboard:

- If available, set the **SEO/meta title**, **meta description**, **focus keyword**, and **slug** per post.
- Set the **permalink/slug** to something short and keyword-rich.
- Add a **featured image** (OG/social image) with alt-text.
- **Canonical:** if the SEO plugin exposes a canonical field, point it at your original; otherwise rework the copy and add a contextual link back.
- ⚠️ Feature availability varies — confirm in your dashboard.
