# AI Citation Targets: EveryCRED

When AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) answer questions about verifiable credentials, digital identity, or government credential systems, they pull from sources across the web. This file tracks the platforms, directories, and content surfaces where EveryCRED needs to be present and well-represented to maximize AI citation frequency.

EveryCRED competes in a market where AI citation matters most for mid-to-late funnel buyers: government IT teams researching solutions, university registrars comparing platforms, and enterprise procurement teams vetting vendors. These buyers ask AI tools before they shortlist vendors.

## How This File Is Used

- **Content writers**: Reference when building external authority into article strategy
- **Marketing team**: Use as an off-page SEO and AI visibility punch list
- **`/research-ai-citations` command**: Generates prompt-specific citation audits that feed back into this file
