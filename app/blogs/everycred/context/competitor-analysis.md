# Competitor Analysis — EveryCRED

This document tracks key competitors in the verifiable credentials and digital identity space, analyzing their product positioning, content strategies, and SEO gaps to identify opportunities for EveryCRED.

**Last Updated:** May 2026
**Next Review:** August 2026
**Reviewer:** Devendra Chande

## EveryCRED Overview (Baseline)

- **Website:** https://www.everycred.com
- **HQ:** India
- **Position:** "#1 Digital Credential Platform" — blockchain-anchored W3C Verifiable Credentials for issuers, holders, and verifiers
- **Notable Clients:** Government of Maharashtra, Raigad Police, Navi Mumbai Police
- **Technology:** SHA-512 blockchain anchoring, zk-SNARKs (ZKPs), W3C VC standards, REST API
- **Industries Served:** Education, Healthcare, Public Sector, Finance, Cybersecurity, Automobile, Supply Chain
- **Key Differentiator:** Only platform with live Indian government deployments + India Stack (DigiLocker/Aadhaar) alignment, Focusing on USA public sector and US government agencies. EveryCRED is official Carahsoft partner.

## Primary Competitors

**Company Overview:**

- **Website:** https://dhiway.com
- **HQ:** Bengaluru, India
- **Founded:** 2019 | **Funding:** $1.51M pre-seed (Cornerstone Venture Partners + SUNiCON Ventures, Nov 2022)
- **Primary Offering:** MARK Studio — credential issuance platform on CORD (proprietary tokenless Layer 1 blockchain). Covers credentials, documents, events, and IoT data with "Continuous Assurance" (credentials remain verifiable indefinitely).
- **Target Audience:** Government, Banking, Fintech, Insurance, Education, Healthcare, Employment
- **Pricing:** Not publicly disclosed — enterprise/government deal-based
- **Market Position:** Only direct India-based competitor; early-stage challenger

**Content Strategy:**

- **Blog URL:** https://dhiway.com/blog
- **Publishing Frequency:** Moderate — ~2–4 posts/month
- **Content Types:** Thought leadership on DPI, digital public infrastructure, Trust over IP, CORD blockchain design, supply chain, telemedicine, QR security
- **Content Quality:** High on technical depth; limited SEO optimization

**Top Performing Content:**

1. CORD blockchain and Digital Public Infrastructure explainer — targets government IT decision-makers
2. 3.1 million student credential issuance case study
3. Supply chain traceability via blockchain

**SEO Strengths:**

- India-native brand — trusted by government buyers
- Deep DPI/Beckn Protocol alignment
- Founding member of Sovrin Foundation and Trust over IP (ToIP)
- OID4VP support (OpenID for Verifiable Presentations)

**SEO Weaknesses:**

- Proprietary CORD chain creates lock-in concern for international buyers
- Limited case studies outside India
- No self-serve pricing — harder for SMBs/universities to onboard
- Blog does not target "verifiable credentials healthcare India" or "automobile" use cases
- Pre-seed stage — limited resources for sustained content output

**Content Gaps:**

- Healthcare credential verification India
- Automobile/vehicle registration verifiable credentials
- Finance/KYC use cases
- Cybersecurity professional certification verification

**Differentiation Opportunities:**

- EveryCRED already has live government deployments vs. Dhiway's claimed partnerships — lead with proof
- Target verticals Dhiway ignores: automobile, cybersecurity, consumer finance
- Multi-vertical content strategy vs. Dhiway's DPI/government focus
- Transparent pricing and self-serve onboarding to capture university/SMB segment

### Competitor 2: Dock.io (now Truvera)

- **HQ:** Zug, Switzerland
- **Founded:** 2017 | **Employees:** ~37

**Content Strategy:**

- **Blog URL:** https://www.dock.io/blog
- **Publishing Frequency:** High — ~4–6 posts/month
- **Content Types:** Ultimate guides (5,000+ words), competitor comparisons, industry use-case explainers, AI agent identity, KYC/AML coverage
- **Average Article Length:** 3,000–6,000 words
- **Content Quality:** Highest of all competitors — comprehensive pillar pages and long-tail coverage

**Top Performing Content:**

1. "Verifiable Credentials: The Ultimate Guide" — targets head keyword "verifiable credentials"
2. "Self-Sovereign Identity: The Ultimate Guide" — targets "self-sovereign identity"
3. "Decentralized Identity: The Complete Guide" — targets "decentralized identity"
4. Competitor comparison articles ("Dock vs. MATTR," etc.) — captures bottom-of-funnel intent
5. "Reusable Identity Vendors Market Review" — comparison / roundup format

**SEO Strengths:**

- Most prolific publisher in the verifiable credentials space
- Owns the top pillar pages for all major head terms
- Transparent pricing (lowest entry barrier: $499/month) — captures self-serve traffic
- Credential monetization model is unique — earns backlinks from fintech bloggers
- AI agent identity content — early ownership of an emerging keyword cluster

**SEO Weaknesses:**

- Zero India presence or India Stack/DigiLocker integration
- No Asia-Pacific market content whatsoever
- Switzerland HQ — limited government trust in regulated Indian markets
- No government client case studies
- Build tier's 250 credential/month limit is unworkable for Indian university deployments (100K+ credentials/year)

**Content Gaps:**

- Verifiable credentials India (any India-specific content)
- DigiLocker, Aadhaar, DPDP Act topics
- Indian government use cases (police, welfare, land records)
- Supply chain India, automobile India
- National Medical Commission (NMC) / healthcare India

**Differentiation Opportunities:**

- Create India-specific versions of every pillar page Dock has (e.g., "Verifiable Credentials India: The Complete Guide")
- Position EveryCRED as the India-first alternative to Dock's global SaaS model
- Use government deployments as case studies — Dock has none
- Publish pricing comparison content (EveryCRED vs. Dock for Indian deployments)
