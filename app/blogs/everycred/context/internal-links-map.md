1. **3–5 internal links per 1,400-word article** — never fewer than 3, never more than 6

3. **Short anchor text** — 2–5 words, natural in sentence, never the full article title
4. **Never use:** "click here," "read more," "you can check this," "review this article"
5. **Prefer specific over general:** Link to the blog post on the exact topic rather than the industry page, unless the industry page is more relevant
